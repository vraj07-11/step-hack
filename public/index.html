Frequently Asked Questions

How does StepHack log steps into Google Fit?
StepHack connects directly to the official Google Fitness REST API using OAuth 2.0. When you input your step count and walk duration, it generates realistic 30-second pedometer intervals with human-like cadence variation, calculating synchronized walking distance (meters) and calories burned before updating your Google Fit timeline.
Why does StepHack distribute steps across time instead of adding a lump sum?
Standard manual activity additions in fitness apps create a single artificial spike that third-party sync engines and health programs often ignore. By distributing steps across realistic 30-second cadence chunks with warm-up and cool-down momentum, Google Fit registers the session as genuine continuous movement.
Do logged steps sync with Pokémon GO Adventure Sync and Health Connect?
Yes. Because StepHack registers data points under phone hardware sensor streams (com.google.step_count.delta on device type phone), Google Fit aggregates the steps into your daily totals, allowing apps that read from Google Fit or Health Connect to recognize the activity.
Is StepHack safe and private to use?
Yes. StepHack operates in compliance with Google API Services User Data Policies. Your authentication tokens are stored exclusively in your browser's encrypted session, and no health datasets are ever stored, indexed, or shared with third parties.
What is the recommended walking cadence for realistic tracking?
Natural human walking pace typically averages between 100 and 115 steps per minute (~4.5 to 5.0 km/h). Use the “Auto-fit pace” feature in the duration slider to automatically calibrate the optimal duration for your step count.
